About

SANORDICA is a modern recruitment and talent consultancy built on a simple principle: the right person can change the direction of a business.

  • That is why we don’t start with CVs.
  • We start with your business.
  • We understand where you are going, what you are building, the challenges you are facing and the kind of people who will make a difference when they join.
  • Then we find them.
  • We combine market knowledge, an international network and a highly personal approach to recruitment—bringing together the right people and the right businesses at the right moment.
  • Because great recruitment isn’t about filling positions.
  • It’s about finding the people who move businesses forward.
